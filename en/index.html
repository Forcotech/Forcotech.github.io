How HSA3 works

Mass-spring principle with resonance cavity

The perforated acoustic meta-metal oscillates over a resonance cavity and converts sound energy into motion. The result: high attenuation with a smooth, open surface – A1 non-combustible and up to 40 % less pressure drop than conventional splitters.

About us

ForCoTech and Silent-Mode were founded by Romolo Vicari. While developing heat pumps in China he realised that the noise of outdoor units can become the biggest obstacle – so he developed the enclosures himself.

The first projects were completed in the United Kingdom in 2015. Since then enclosures have been installed all over Europe, and in smaller numbers in the USA and as far as Australia. Since 2024 Silent Engineering di Romolo Vicari, based in Osnago (Italy), continues the development.

Our design work relies on digital tools – AI, BIM and CFD. Manufacturing is carried out in alliance with ABSORA GmbH, Berlin.

FAQ

Which equipment do you build enclosures for?

For air-to-water and large heat pumps, chillers, dry coolers, VRF/VRV outdoor units, PV inverters and battery storage (BESS), also for data centres – single enclosures or one shared enclosure for several units in cascade.

How much noise reduction is possible?

The Standard version achieves approx. −15 dB(A), the Premium version approx. −20 dB(A). For each project the enclosure is designed to meet the noise limits at the relevant receiver point.

What does an enclosure cost?

Standard enclosures for PV inverters start at approx. €4,400 net, standard enclosures for VRF/VRV outdoor units at approx. €15,500 net (excl. installation, VAT and freight). For the H-Series and the best-selling large units, the catalogue pages in the configurator show fixed list prices. All other enclosures are engineered per project – you receive the price with the quotation, for complex projects usually within 2–3 days.

Does it work with R290 (propane)?

Yes, with written approval from the unit manufacturer and a floorless design that lets leaked propane flow away freely at the bottom. No ignition sources inside the enclosure (except explosion-proof equipment), permanently open ventilation openings; where required, gas detection with explosion-proof forced ventilation.

Does the enclosure reduce efficiency?

Air flow and pressure drop are designed with CFD simulation; air velocity is limited and checked against the fan's available external pressure so the loss of capacity stays at max. 3–5 %. HSA3 has up to 40 % less pressure drop than conventional splitters.

How long do manufacturing and delivery take?

Custom enclosures are usually delivered within 40–60 days from receipt of order. For complex projects you will usually receive the binding quotation within 2–3 days; the delivery date is stated in the quotation.

Why don’t you name reference projects?

Once delivered, the installations belong to our customers. We therefore do not publish customer names or locations without their express consent. Our neutralised sample quotations show what our installations look like.
