Technology and design

An enclosure must never choke the equipment. That is why we design acoustics and air flow together and verify both before anything is built.

  • CFD simulation of air flow and pressure drop – no air short-circuit, no loss of capacity
  • Noise calculation from sound power, distance and type of area
  • BIM model for coordination with designers and site management
  • Structural design and wind load to EN 1991-1-4, also for rooftop installation
  • Easy maintenance: doors and removable panels for service and replacement

Construction

Robust, modular and adaptable later on.

  • Frame of anodised aluminium profiles
  • Powder-coated panels in any RAL colour
  • Inner lining of HSA3 (Absora technology), non-combustible A1, with resonance cavity
  • Tube silencers in the roof for upward air discharge
  • No floor and no contact with the unit – structure-borne noise is not transferred to the enclosure

About us

ForCoTech and Silent-Mode were founded by Romolo Vicari. While developing heat pumps in China he realised that the noise of outdoor units can become the biggest obstacle – so he developed the enclosures himself.

The first projects were completed in the United Kingdom in 2015. Since then enclosures have been installed all over Europe, and in smaller numbers in the USA and as far as Australia. Since 2024 Silent Engineering di Romolo Vicari, based in Osnago (Italy), continues the development.

Our design work relies on digital tools – AI, BIM and CFD. Manufacturing uses Absora technology.

FAQ

Which equipment do you build enclosures for?

For air-to-water and large heat pumps, chillers, dry coolers, VRF/VRV outdoor units and PV inverters – single enclosures or one shared enclosure for several units in cascade.

How much noise reduction is possible?

The Standard version achieves approx. −15 dB(A), the Premium version approx. −20 dB(A). For each project the enclosure is designed to meet the noise limits at the relevant receiver point.

What does an enclosure cost?

Standard enclosures for PV inverters start at approx. €4,550 net, standard enclosures for VRF/VRV outdoor units at approx. €15,500 net (excl. installation, VAT and freight). The configurator gives a budget price for any other equipment.

Does it work with R290 (propane)?

Yes. The manufacturer's safety zone is taken into account in the design; where required, a gas detection system with explosion-proof forced ventilation is included.

Does the enclosure reduce efficiency?

Air flow and pressure drop are designed with CFD simulation; air velocity is limited and checked against the fan's available external pressure so the unit runs without loss of capacity.
