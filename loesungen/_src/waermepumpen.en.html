The problem

Why the outdoor unit becomes a dispute at night

During the day, a heat pump's noise blends into everyday sounds. At night, when the surroundings are quiet and the unit runs at full load in winter, it is clearly audible – often right outside the neighbour's bedroom window.

In Germany the relevant standard is the TA Lärm: the level is assessed 0.5 m in front of the open window of the nearest noise-sensitive room. At night (10 pm – 6 am) the limits are considerably stricter than during the day.

  • Night limits (Germany): 35 dB(A) in purely residential areas, 40 dB(A) in general residential areas, 45 dB(A) in mixed and village areas.
  • Location: In front of a wall about 3 dB is added, in a corner or recess about 6 dB.
  • Distance: Each doubling of distance gains only about 6 dB – hardly usable on small plots.
  • Tonality: If a distinct tone can be heard, a penalty of 3 or 6 dB applies.
  • Several units: Two equally loud outdoor units are together 3 dB louder than one.

Worked example

Outdoor unit with a sound power of 60 dB(A), 5 m to the neighbour's window, installed in a building corner: about 44 dB(A) arrives at the window. In a general residential area 40 dB(A) is allowed at night – the unit is 4 dB too loud. With an enclosure at level Basic (−10 dB(A)) the level drops to about 34 dB(A), safely below the limit.

The solution

Quiet, without holding the heat pump back

An enclosure that suffocates the unit helps nobody: the heat pump loses capacity, the fan speeds up and gets louder again. That is why the H-Series is an air-flow design first – and a sound insulation second.

Slow air

All grilles have 75 % free area and are designed for a maximum of 4 m/s. No flow noise, and the fan does not need to compensate.

No short-circuit

An HSA3 air separation divides intake and discharge. The cold exhaust air does not return to the evaporator.

Matching air inlet

Intake via the roof at the rear, at the side rear or centrally in the back wall; discharge at the front side, left or right – depending on the location.

HSA3 inside

The acoustic meta-metal lining also damps the low-frequency hum of the compressor. Non-combustible A1, no fibres.

Floorless

The enclosure stands over the unit without touching it. Vibration is not transmitted, and it can be retrofitted at any time.

Fits the house

Panels of powder-coated aluminium in any RAL colour, frame anodised or in panel colour. Service doors for maintenance.

Frequently asked questions

Does the heat pump lose capacity inside the enclosure?

Not noticeably, as long as the air can flow freely. That is why all H-Series grilles are designed for a maximum of 4 m/s in the free cross-section, and an air separation prevents the cold exhaust air from being drawn in again.

Which noise reduction level do I need?

That depends on the unit's sound power, the distance to the nearest window, the location (free-standing, in front of a wall, in a corner) and the area type. The configurator calculates it and suggests the most economical level that meets the night limit with a margin.

Can the enclosure be placed over an existing heat pump?

Yes. The enclosure has no floor and stands around the unit without touching it. It is placed over the existing outdoor unit; the pipework stays as it is.

Can the unit still be serviced?

Yes. Service doors and removable panels give access to connections and the control panel. For larger work, the enclosure can be dismantled – it is bolted, not welded.

What if my unit does not fit a standard size?

Then we build to measure. The configurator shows this and sends us the unit data, required reduction and installation situation; we come back with a quotation.

Is the enclosure also suitable for AC units and pool heat pumps?

Yes. What matters are the dimensions and the air flow of the outdoor unit, not the type of system. Split AC units, multi-split outdoor units and pool heat pumps usually fit a standard size.
