Data centres

Free-standing acoustic packs for chillers and dry coolers on the roof: Helmholtz tube silencers above the fans, pressure drop within the unit reserve.

View solution → Material

HSA3 – the material in every enclosure

Acoustic meta-metal instead of mineral wool, reverberation room tested: fibre-free up to αₛ 0.80, with wool backing 0.83 already at 100 Hz. Non-combustible A1.
