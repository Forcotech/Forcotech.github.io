Air routing to suit the site

The grilles have 75 % free area and are sized so that the air never flows faster than 4 m/s. HSA3 sheets separate the suction and pressure chambers – discharged air is not drawn in again and the heat pump keeps its performance.

Roof, rear

Air enters at the top rear. Ideal right against the building wall or in tight corners.

Side, rear

Air inlet right, left or on both sides at the rear. Keep 500 mm clear at the side.

Back wall, centre

Air inlet from behind. For free-standing enclosures at least 500 mm from the wall.

The air outlet is always at the side, front – either left or right, so the air can be directed away from the neighbours.

Frequently asked questions

How loud may my heat pump be at the neighbour's window at night?

In Germany the TA Lärm applies. At night the limits at the neighbour's window are 35 dB(A) in purely residential areas, 40 dB(A) in general residential areas and 45 dB(A) in mixed or village areas. For heat pumps it is recommended to stay at least 6 dB below that. Other countries have their own rules; the configurator calculates the level at the neighbour's window with and without the enclosure.

Does the heat pump lose performance inside the enclosure?

Not if the air routing is right. All grilles are designed for a maximum of 4 m/s and the suction and pressure chambers are separated. The configurator shows the pressure drop of the enclosure and compares it with the fan's pressure reserve. If it is close to the limit, please check with the unit manufacturer.

What if no standard size fits?

Then we build the enclosure to measure. The configurator detects this automatically and sends us everything we need for the design with your enquiry: unit, air flow, sound power, location and required level reduction.

Is the enclosure suitable for heat pumps with R290 (propane)?

Yes. The enclosure has no floor and the base profiles have through-flow openings. In case of a leak the heavy refrigerant flows away at ground level instead of building up inside the enclosure. Keep the protection zone specified by the unit manufacturer as usual.

Can I reach the unit for maintenance?

Yes. Every enclosure has removable service doors, the twin enclosure one per unit. The enclosure does not have to be dismantled for maintenance or repairs.

Do two heat pumps fit into one enclosure?

Yes, as a twin enclosure for two units side by side, 800 mm apart. The configurator then uses the air flow and sound power of both units.

Which colours are available?

The panels are powder-coated in any RAL colour. The frame is anodised as standard and can also be RAL-coated for a surcharge.
